Use `renderGlyphChartMermaid` for all three families. Omit render `height` to let these structured layouts choose their content height; fixed heights can omit content and report it.

## Gantt schedules

Dates and `after` dependencies produce calendar intervals. Forward references are supported. Status tags such as `done`, `active`, and `crit` remain identifiable; milestones become dots when needed.

<GlyphRender kind="chart-mermaid" source={"gantt\n title Portal release\n dateFormat YYYY-MM-DD\n axisFormat %b %d\n section Discovery\n Research :done, research, 2026-04-06, 3d\n Prototype :design, after research, 4d\n section Delivery\n API :active, api, after design, 5d\n Launch :milestone, after api, 0d"} options={{"target": "chat", "width": 72, "height": 20}} />

Calendar ticks use local dates and can coarsen to fit. Excluded days are not shaded; today/layout settings may remain inert with notes. Supply complete dates and task starts when their meaning matters; partial or missing dates can use logged epoch defaults.

## Journey scores and actors

Scores render as compact 0–5 bars with task and actor text, grouped by section. Out-of-range scores are clamped with notes.

## Timeline events

Periods and events form a table in source order, rather than a quantitative time axis. Whitespace-followed colons split events; embedded times and URLs retain their colons.

<GlyphRender kind="chart-mermaid" source={"timeline\n title Portal roadmap\n section Foundation\n April : Interviews : Prototype\n May : API beta\n section Delivery\n June : Private preview\n July : Public launch : Support training"} options={{"target": "chat", "width": 68, "height": 20}} />

Long event text wraps on words. Narrow layouts can stack the event column. Increase width or inspect omission notes when a fixed size cannot retain all entries. For ownership lanes and dependencies without dates, see [Git history and lane diagrams](/diagrams/gitgraph/).
