## Syntax and limits

- Braced member rows form compartments below the class header. Visibility markers and generic type text remain literal.
- Inheritance and realization use triangle heads; aggregation and composition use open and filled diamonds.
- Associations, dependencies, relationship labels, quoted multiplicities, namespaces, and notes are adapted into graph nodes, groups, and captions.
- Class identifiers may contain hyphens. Spaced generic types are accepted.
- Configuration, styles, and URLs are inert and reported. Cell budgets can wrap or omit captions: inspect the fidelity report and [increase the budget](/diagrams/options/).
