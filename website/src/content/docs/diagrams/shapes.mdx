## ASCII and font compatibility

`ascii` uses simpler outlines. Box, blocks, and braille hexagons use ❬ and ❭ sides;
ASCII uses angle brackets. Clouds and bangs also have ASCII outlines.

<GlyphRender kind="diagram" source={{"direction": "LR", "nodes": [{"id": "n", "label": "Decision", "shape": "hexagon"}], "edges": []}} options={{"target": "chat", "charset": "ascii", "width": 30, "height": 9}} />

Use Glyph Mono for special glyphs in web output; chat uses the receiving application's font.
See [fonts and fallbacks](/diagrams/troubleshooting/#fonts-and-alignment).

## Adaptations and connection limits

Catalogue shapes reserve their full footprint and attach edges away from seams.
`point` draws the first character of its required nonempty label and cannot have a direct JSON self-edge. `fork` draws an
unlabelled bar/stub; a supplied label goes below it. `compartment` accepts member rows.

Mermaid names `lin-doc`, `win-pane`, `sl-rect`, and `folder` have logged adaptations;
they are not additional JSON shape values. External icons and images are inert.
Inspect render notes when [fitting](/diagrams/options/) changes an outline or label.
