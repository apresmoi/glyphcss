## Syntax and limits

- `columns N` sets a grid. `:N` spans that many columns, including the gaps between them.
- `block:Name ... end` nests a grid. `space` reserves blank cells; spaces and empty block input are supported.
- Labels, shapes, and arrows become graph elements. Styling and external assets are inert.
- Crowded grids may wrap to fewer columns (`block-row-wrap`) or use the general fitter (`block-layout`). Column positions are hints rather than fixed pixel coordinates.
- Large or tall blocks can paginate. Expansion and explicit direction are available through [render options](/diagrams/options/).
