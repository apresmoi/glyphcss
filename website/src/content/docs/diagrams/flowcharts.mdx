## Syntax and limits

- Declare labels with `A[Label]`, decisions with `A{Question}`, and storage with `A[(Records)]`. See the [shape catalogue](/diagrams/shapes/) for JSON and `@{shape: ...}` forms.
- Use `-->` for a directed edge, `---` for a line, `-.->` for dotted, and `==>` for thick. Pipe labels such as `-->|yes|` travel with their edge.
- `subgraph ... end` creates a group; nested groups reserve enclosing frames.
- Bidirectional edges retain both heads. Invisible connections influence layout without painting an edge.
- Styling, click actions, images, and external assets do not execute. Read `mermaid-degraded` notes for adaptations.
- Long labels wrap. A fixed grid may simplify or split a graph; choose [faithful detail or expansion](/diagrams/options/) when identity matters.
