## Syntax and limits

- Entity declarations can include typed attribute rows and `PK`, `FK`, or `UK` markers. Backtick identifiers and comments in attribute syntax remain text.
- `||`, `o|`, `|{`, and `o{` encode one, zero-or-one, one-or-more, and zero-or-more endpoints. Crow-foot heads carry the distinction without color.
- Solid/dotted relationships and captions are adapted to graph edges. Attributes are displayed, not validated as database types.
- Styles and URLs are inert. Wide attribute lists need more columns or rows; use [faithful detail](/diagrams/options/#detail-levels) to retain identities before splitting.
