## Syntax and limits

- Requirement declarations include `requirement`, `functionalRequirement`, `interfaceRequirement`, `performanceRequirement`, `physicalRequirement`, and `designConstraint`.
- Fields include `id`, `text`, `risk`, and `verifymethod`. Elements include `type` and `docref`.
- Relations are `contains`, `copies`, `derives`, `satisfies`, `verifies`, `refines`, and `traces`.
- Markdown is reduced to plain text; styles and external assets are inert and reported.
- Long field text wraps inside compartments. Give detailed requirements more rows and inspect [render notes](/diagrams/options/#read-the-fidelity-report) before sharing.
